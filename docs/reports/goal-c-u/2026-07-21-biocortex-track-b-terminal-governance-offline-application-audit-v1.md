only read-only inspection of versioned synthetic packets, deterministic replay
training, and static integration linting. They do not establish a production
memory system, biological-brain model, provider correctness, or authority to
operate any real control plane.

coverage label, enabling network or real evidence, weakening a forbidden
capability, or changing the fixture hash fails closed.

## Explainability boundary

policy rule matched, and rejection reason. The chain cannot explain real-world
causal outcomes, data quality outside fixtures, biological cognition, production
availability, or provider/runtime correctness.
